1.12 · Aesthetic

Vaporwave.

Pastel marble pinks and cyans, Greco-Roman busts, MTV-era 90s typography, ironic Japanese subtitles. The internet's most beloved ironic-luxury aesthetic.

What it is

Vaporwave is a music genre that became a visual aesthetic — pastel pinks and cyans, Greco-Roman statues, Japanese characters, glitchy chrome type, and faded 1990s shopping-mall imagery, all assembled with intentional irony. It originated on Tumblr in the early 2010s and is now the default look for nostalgia-themed indie sites, Bandcamp pages, NFT projects, and merch drops.

How it works

Background combines large pastel radial gradients (hot pink + cyan blooms on a marble-cream base) with a faint scanline overlay (repeating-linear-gradient at very low opacity). Typography uses a condensed display sans (Anton, Bebas Neue) at huge sizes with multi-layered offset color shadows — a magenta shadow + a cyan shadow at different offsets gives the chromatic-aberration look. Japanese characters in Noto Serif JP, set vertically or as small subtitles, complete the aesthetic. A Greek bust (real image or a CSS sculpture) anchors the composition.

Prompt · 1.12 Vaporwave 
Build a vaporwave hero section. Import Anton (condensed display sans) and Noto Serif JP from Google Fonts. Background: stack a pastel radial gradient (hot pink #ff77c8 at bottom-left, cyan #7be0ff at top-right) over a cream-marble base (linear gradient pink → light blue). Add a faint scanline overlay using ::after with repeating-linear-gradient at 2px stripes, 4% opacity. Add a marble overlay using ::before with radial gradients in white and black at low opacity, mix-blend-mode:overlay. Headline in Anton, uppercase, font-size clamp(50px,8vw,88px), with a chromatic-aberration text-shadow stack: 4px 4px 0 #ff2ec4, 8px 8px 0 #7be0ff, plus a soft magenta blur. Add vertical Japanese characters in Noto Serif JP at the top-right corner (writing-mode:vertical-rl). Place a circular "marble bust" element (radial gradient sculpting + inset shadows + a 𓁹 unicode glyph) on the right side of the hero. CTA pill: hot pink background, white border, hard offset white shadow. The whole effect should feel like a 1996 shopping mall poster.
