6.3 · UI Pattern

Testimonials /
Marquee.

Two rows scrolling infinitely in opposite directions. Hover any card to pause the row.

What it is

A section showing quotes from real users of your product. Social proof is one of the most powerful conversion elements on a website — people trust other people's opinions more than they trust the company selling the product. The best testimonials are specific ("increased our revenue by 40%") rather than generic ("great product, love it").

How it works

Laid out as a 3-column grid, a single large quote, or an auto-scrolling marquee. The marquee version is popular currently — cards scroll continuously, often two rows in opposite directions. Hover stops the scroll. Each card duplicated once for the infinite-loop illusion via translateX(-50%) keyframes.

Prompt · 6.3 Testimonials Marquee 
Create a testimonial section with a dual-row infinite marquee. Two rows of testimonial cards, each row auto-scrolling horizontally at different speeds. Row 1 scrolls left at 30px/s, Row 2 scrolls right at 20px/s. Each card (280px wide, white background, 12px border-radius, subtle shadow): star rating (5 gold stars), quote text in quotes, horizontal divider, avatar circle (40px, initials), name (bold 14px), role and company (12px gray). 8 cards per row duplicated for infinite loop (use CSS animation with keyframes translating X by -50%). Section background: light gray #F5F5F5.

  • Testimonial.toAnother testimonial widget product — great reference for modern UI
